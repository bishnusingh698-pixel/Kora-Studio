What SourceTrac is

SourceTrac is a survey app for Shopify, published by SourceTrac Survey. It adds a short survey to your Thank-you page, and optionally to the Order status page, and shows you which channels bring in revenue.

You may use SourceTrac if you are a Shopify merchant who installs it, and if you are allowed to agree to these terms for your store.

Plans and billing

SourceTrac has a Free plan and the paid Growth and Scale plans. The features of each plan and the prices are set out on the Pricing page.

Paid plans are billed through Shopify. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice alongside your other app subscriptions. SourceTrac never stores your card details. Shopify handles the payment and tells us when a subscription starts, changes or ends.

Yearly billing costs 10% less than paying monthly and is charged once a year. An upgrade, or a switch to yearly, replaces your current charge once you approve it in Shopify. A downgrade, or a switch from yearly to monthly, starts when your current billing period ends.

Prices are in USD.

Changing or cancelling your plan

You can change or cancel any time on the Plans page.

If you cancel, your answers stay saved and Shopify credits the unused part of your billing period. On the Free plan only your top live survey keeps showing to buyers, Shopify tags, metafields and Flow stop, and answers past 50 in a month are kept but marked over the limit.

If you reach the Free plan's monthly answer limit, answers keep being collected, stored and counted in your dashboard totals. Nothing is hidden. Upgrading only removes the monthly limit.

You are responsible for your store and for the questions your survey asks. Ask questions that do not invite personal details such as names or emails.

You are responsible for mentioning the survey in your own store's privacy policy. Because you decide what your survey asks, you are the controller of your buyers' data and SourceTrac processes it for you.

You must only use SourceTrac in the way Shopify and the law allow.
