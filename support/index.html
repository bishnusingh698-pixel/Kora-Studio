Before you write

The fastest fix for a missing survey is usually the checkout editor check.

  • The survey is live in SourceTrac.
  • The “SourceTrac survey” block is on the Thank you page in Shopify's checkout editor (Settings › Checkout › Customize, then Thank you in the page menu), and the editor is saved.
  • You are testing with a new order, because each order is asked only once.
  • Your store is not on the Shopify Starter plan.
  • For the Order status page: your store uses Shopify's new customer accounts, and the block is added on that page too.

Tip. In the app, Surveys › Checkout check and Recent checkouts show why a survey did or didn't show on a recent order.

More answers are on the Help and FAQ page.
