What SourceTrac does

SourceTrac adds a short survey to your Thank-you page, and optionally to the Order status page. Each answer is tied to its order, so you see orders, revenue and average order value per channel, kept separate for each currency. Start from a ready-made template or write your own questions with follow-ups. Export answers to CSV. On Scale, answers can tag orders and customers, set a customer metafield, or start a Shopify Flow workflow.

Get started

How it works

Four steps, and you only set up the checkout block once.

  1. Create your survey.

    Pick a template, adjust the questions in your own words and press Go live.

  2. Add the block to your Thank-you page.

    In Shopify's checkout editor, choose Thank you from the page menu at the top, add the “SourceTrac survey” block and press Save. You only do this once.

  3. Answer it with a test order.

    Place a test order and answer the survey on the Thank-you page. Each order is asked only once, so use a new order for every test. Your answer appears here within a minute.

  4. Read your results.

    Results groups answers by question and shows the revenue behind each channel.
