How billing works

Charges appear on your Shopify invoice alongside your other app subscriptions. SourceTrac never stores your card details — Shopify handles the payment and tells us when a subscription starts, changes or ends.

Yearly billing

Yearly billing costs 10% less than paying monthly and is charged once a year. An upgrade, or a switch to yearly, replaces your current charge once you approve it in Shopify. A downgrade, or a switch from yearly to monthly, starts when your current billing period ends.

If you reach the Free limit

Answers keep being collected, stored and counted in your dashboard totals. Nothing is hidden. Upgrading only removes the monthly limit.

If you cancel

Your answers stay saved. Shopify credits the unused part of your billing period. On Free only your top live survey keeps showing to buyers, Shopify tags, metafields and Flow stop, and answers past 50 this month are kept but marked over the limit.
