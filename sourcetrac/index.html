How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes in total.

  1. Pick a survey

    Pick a ready-made survey, such as "How did you hear about us?", or write your own questions.

  2. Add the block in Shopify

    Add the SourceTrac block to your Thank-you page in Shopify's checkout editor. The Order status page is an optional extra.

  3. See revenue per channel

    Each answer is tied to its order, so you see orders, revenue and average order value per channel.

Features

  • One-tap survey on the Thank-you page, with an optional Order status page block
  • Orders, revenue and average order value per channel, kept separate for each currency (never mixed)
  • Ready-made templates, follow-up questions that show based on earlier answers, and NPS and CSAT scores
  • Dashboard, channel report and results per survey, with 7, 30 and 90 day views
  • CSV export of every answer
  • On the Scale plan: tag orders and customers from answers, set a customer metafield, and start a Shopify Flow workflow
  • Available in 10 admin languages, and surveys can be shown to buyers in their language

Privacy in short

SourceTrac stores the order, the answer and when it was given, plus the order total and currency. It never stores a buyer's name, email, phone number or address.

Works with Shopify stores on any plan except Shopify Starter, where Shopify does not run checkout blocks. The Order status page block needs new customer accounts.

FAQ

Does it slow down checkout?

No. The survey appears on the Thank-you page, after the order is placed, and never blocks the page.

What if an answer arrives before the order?

It is saved straight away and shows as Pending until Shopify sends the order, then the revenue fills in.

Do refunds count?

No. Refunded, cancelled and test orders are left out of revenue.

Can I customise the colours?

The survey uses your store's own checkout styling automatically. You control the question text and the emoji on each answer.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Your store's data is deleted when Shopify asks us to after uninstall, as Shopify requires.
