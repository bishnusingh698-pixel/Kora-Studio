This policy explains what data Kora Studio collects when you use our apps, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We have kept it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

Kora Studio is [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], at [CONTACT ADDRESS]. You can contact us at sourcetracsupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • The order ID and order number.
  • The buyer's survey answers and when they were given.
  • The order total, currency and payment status from Shopify.
  • The store's own settings.

We do not collect buyers' names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.

Why we collect it

To show the merchant which channels and answers relate to their orders and revenue.

Shopify actions

On the Scale plan only, and only when the merchant turns them on, answers can be written back to the merchant's own Shopify store as order tags, customer tags or a customer metafield.

How long we keep it

  • Survey answers and order records: 24 months.
  • Technical webhook records: 30 days.
  • Access credentials are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Deletion and requests

We act on Shopify's customer data request, customer redaction and shop redaction requests. Merchants can also contact us to ask for data to be deleted.
